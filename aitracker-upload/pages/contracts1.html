Why the Cerebras deal matters most

At up to $2.5B, the Cerebras agreement dwarfs everything else DGXX has signed and is the main reason FY2027 guidance jumped to $250–300M. It also validates the company's pivot from Bitcoin mining into "vertically integrated" AI infra — owning its own power and land, not just leasing space.

Execution risk to watch

DGXX is a micro/small-cap (market cap roughly $500–700M, depending on the day) with high share-price volatility — 52-week range has spanned roughly $1.86 to $9.20. Phase 1 of the Alabama campus, NVIDIA Vera Rubin early-access delivery, and further customer announcements are the near-term catalysts to watch.

What the verification badges actually mean
Three honest categories, replacing the old "Verified" / "Unverified" split
Confirmed from primary source

Someone has opened the actual press release, 6-K, or 8-K and read the contract terms directly. The "Source" column links to the specific filing or release, not a search query.

Reported, not yet opened

Multiple reputable outlets (CNBC, Reuters, TipRanks, etc.) report this figure consistently, but nobody has cross-checked it against the underlying filing yet. Treat terms as probably right, not certain.

Unverified headline signal

Surfaced by the live scanners below from news headlines or filing titles. Could be a real new deal, could be a rumor, could be noise. Nothing here is confirmed until you open the source.

Its only disclosed infrastructure contract is the DGXX deal above; it has not signed or disclosed any other capacity agreement as of this page's last update. Its own AI model claims (a 12-million-token context window) are unrelated to infrastructure spend and are unverified by third parties — not something this page tracks.
Why the Cerebras deal matters most

At up to $2.5B, the Cerebras agreement dwarfs everything else DGXX has signed and is the main reason FY2027 guidance jumped to $250–300M. The stock closed up roughly 29% the day it was announced (intraday highs near +40%) — by far the largest single-day move of any contract tracked on this page. It also validates the company's pivot from Bitcoin mining into "vertically integrated" AI infra — owning its own power and land, not just leasing space.

Execution risk to watch

DGXX is a micro/small-cap with high share-price volatility — shares have moved from roughly $1.86 to north of $9 over the 2026 rally. Phase 1 of the Alabama campus (targeted online mid-December 2026), NVIDIA Vera Rubin early-access delivery, and further customer announcements are the near-term catalysts to watch.

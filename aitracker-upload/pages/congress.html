Congressional stock trading tracker

Members of Congress must disclose personal stock trades within 45 days under the STOCK Act. This isn't insider information — it's public, lagging, and dollar-range only — but it's a real, free, and legal data feed people watch (the "Pelosi tracker" pattern).

i
Good to know before you read this as a signal: disclosures lag the actual trade by up to 45 days, amounts are reported as ranges (not exact dollar figures) not precise numbers, and a member's trade may be made by a spouse or through a blind trust they don't actively direct. None of this is real-time and none of it is advice to mirror.
